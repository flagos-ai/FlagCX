ascend/shmem: run the unified-IR kernels on one vector core

The vendor's transport kernels are MIX kernels that drive aclshmem from AIV
only (barrier_all_kernel.cpp:16-31 puts behind ASCEND_IS_AIV + sub-block 0;
mte/sdma are vector-core engines). Our bare __aicore__ kernels land on the
cube core: the puts are issued, completion is not tracked, and np=2 hangs at
S16 on rank 1 right after rank 0 reads its own ticket.

core_ratio(0, 1) pins each kernel to a single vector core, the smallest
shape that matches the vendor's without pulling in the AscendC headers the
ASCEND_IS_AIV guard needs.
