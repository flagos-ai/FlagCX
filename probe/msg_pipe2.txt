ascend/shmem: retire local GM stores before the engine reads them

pipe_barrier at kernel entry was too early: the stale read is the scratch
word written two instructions before the put, not the FFTS config. Move the
barrier into the put wrapper so every transfer whose source is a local store
(putValue's scratch, the barrier tickets) retires those stores first, while
transfers from prepared buffers pay one pipe barrier.

Measured symptom: np=2, S18B combo 0 arrives as 0 on rank 0 in 4 of 15
sizes, always the first putValue of the kernel and never combos 1-5.
